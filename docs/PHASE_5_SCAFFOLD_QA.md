# Phase 5 — Scaffold Artifacts & Case-Study QA

**Status:** Complete  
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Objective
Strengthen Scaffold as the portfolio’s primary evidence of end-to-end UX research practice without overstating what five exploratory interviews and moderated prototype walkthroughs establish.

## Changes completed
- Preserved the approved case-study architecture: context → scope → method → synthesis → prototype response → evaluation → ethics → limitations → change → next steps.
- Retained the research-pattern model in place of the Alex persona and clarified the evidentiary rationale.
- Regenerated all four public research artifacts from their source PDFs so the HTML no longer silently compresses source content.
- Added a visible artifact note separating source material from website editorial framing.
- Retained original PDFs as secondary downloads.

## Artifact hierarchy
1. **Research Synthesis** — highest-value supporting artifact because it demonstrates movement from interview material to design implications.
2. **Usability Findings + Iteration Decisions** — demonstrates evaluation and response to observed interaction patterns.
3. **Interview Guide** — demonstrates planning, scope, participant framing, and question design.
4. **Ethical AI Constraints** — demonstrates how autonomy and consent became product constraints rather than after-the-fact principles.

## Hiring rationale
Scaffold should be judged primarily on whether a hiring manager can trace a line from research question to evidence to synthesis to design response. The revised architecture makes that chain explicit and places limitations beside the work rather than hiding them.

## Evidence boundaries
- Five interviews remain exploratory evidence, not population-level validation.
- Participant criteria are based on self-identified lived experience rather than diagnosis alone.
- Prototype walkthroughs are not represented as longitudinal product validation.
- Clinical claims are outside the project’s evidence.
- AI feasibility, privacy architecture, and real-world safety remain unresolved implementation questions.

## Acceptance criteria
- The research question, role, methods, participant scope, synthesis, design response, evaluation, ethics, limitations, and next steps are visible on the case-study page.
- Supporting artifact pages preserve source content in HTML.
- The Alex persona is not presented as a validated user archetype.
- Design decisions trace to observed or reported patterns.
- Original PDFs remain downloadable.
- No new empirical claims were introduced during implementation.
