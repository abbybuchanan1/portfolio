# Phase 4 — Research Memo Conversion & Editorial QA

**Status:** Complete  
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Decision
All four research memos remain in the portfolio as working memos. Their role is to demonstrate exploratory synthesis and question formation, not completed empirical findings.

## Source-fidelity correction
The first HTML conversion compressed portions of the source PDFs. The HTML pages have now been regenerated to preserve the full memo prose and abstracts rather than silently abridging them. The only added editorial element is an **Evidence status** section, visually separated from the authored memo text.

## Publication decisions
- **Memo 01 — The Invisible Tax:** Retain. Strongest direct connection to the portfolio’s translation-burden thesis.
- **Memo 02 — The Real AI Divide Isn’t Intelligence. It’s Bandwidth.:** Retain. Extends the cognitive-bandwidth question and connects directly to participation.
- **Memo 03 — The Opportunity Gap:** Retain, but secondary to Memos 01 and 02 on the homepage. It asks a distinct institutional question: who retains the capacity created by efficiency.
- **Memo 04 — The Consequence Gap:** Retain on the Research page, not the homepage. It demonstrates broad systems reasoning but is less directly tied to UX practice and therefore remains tertiary evidence.

## Evidence discipline
The memos are labeled **Working memo · Provisional**. No memo is described as a study, finding, or validated framework. No outside evidence has been silently added during migration.

## Acceptance criteria
- Full source prose is represented in HTML.
- Original PDFs remain secondary downloads.
- Memo numbering, titles, dates, and authorship are preserved.
- Exploratory status is explicit.
- The memos are hierarchically differentiated rather than presented as equal hiring evidence.
- No memo is promoted as empirical research.
